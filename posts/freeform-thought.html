Freeform Thought

"There is an unaffordable opportunity cost to indecision."

July 11, 2026 2 min read

I've recently become acquainted with this phrase, "There is an unaffordable opportunity cost to indecision." I read the first chapter of Don Quixote a while ago. The behemoth of a book became tattered in the rain, and I haven't touched it since. However, it was clear the message was not to take life too seriously and to take action. Quixote spends his days reading of adventures until one day, for no particular reason, as if an aimlessly wondering thought had just entered his mind, he decides to buy a noble steed and the finest armor. Neither of which are truly noble or finest in any sense, as the horse is a stunted donkey and the armor is rusted and overfitting.

I am afraid of the future in many ways. But I also know I have a verse to contribute. My path is unique and if I continue to walk it, I will create a novel concept in this world. Even if AI can synthesize all the data in the world there are infinite creative ideas that can be generated and you need subject matter expertise integrated into the AI to develop novelty.

Yesterday in my journal I considered what it would be like if we were wandering the cosmos in bionic bodies, our brains being sustained for millennia. Maybe that is humanity's collective future. I wonder if there still need to be advances in how the models are trained to self-train.

Each day I study a concept I find a new, stronger way to encode it. The active processes of my brain's information intake system can make a vast difference on the strength of encoding.

The goal, now, is to keep building on GitHub where my interests align in biotechnology of memory and understanding. And to treat my life more like an academic adventure into the cosmos of the human body. I read about how there are particles we have no understanding of other than electrons, protons, neutrons. And how quantum medicine will eventually become a field. I'll continue to learn about our somatic universe for as long as I possibly can. And yet also find time to enjoy the fact that I am alive and understand very little in the scheme of everything. To search for truths through experiment and developing a unique expertise and understanding.
